Plutus terms of use

Last updated 28 October 2026.

What Plutus is

Plutus is a personal, non-commercial Android app that shows the balance and transactions of a bank account its user connects. It is built and used by its developer for their own account and is not offered as a service to anyone else.

Read-only access

Plutus only reads account information, through Enable Banking, after you give consent at your bank. It cannot make payments. You can withdraw access at any time with Disconnect in the app or at your bank. Enable Banking's own terms apply to its service.

Your data

Your data stays on your phone, as described in the privacy policy.

No warranty

Plutus is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. Categories, totals, fixed-cost detection and "left to spend" are estimates and may be wrong or incomplete; they are not financial advice. Your bank's own records are what count. The developer is not liable for decisions made using the app.
